## Script

**00:00-01:00 | Sofia**
Welcome everyone. Goal today: align on winter-readiness performance, inventory pressure points, and customer-impact mitigation before next snowfall window.

**01:00-02:30 | Amrita**
Store 214 completed 43 tire appointments Friday, average wait 24 minutes. We protected seven at-risk bookings after expedited top-up. Remaining risk is narrow inventory on 235/55R19 by Thursday.

**02:30-04:00 | Jordan**
Auto Service overall completion is 92.5% this week. We moved 14 appointments and had 2 no-shows. I recommend keeping the CAD 25 service credit policy for moves over 48 hours.

**04:00-05:30 | David**
Store 118 had lower booking pressure but our front-end queue spiked when weather alerts hit. Suggest district-wide templated SMS for revised appointment windows.

**05:30-07:00 | Priya**
Store 327 is stable on bookings but not on battery inventory accuracy. We found a 4-unit variance Thursday. We need daily cycle count on top 25 winter SKUs this week.

**07:00-08:30 | Sofia**
Decision check:
1) Keep proactive 24h + 2h reminders.
2) Keep CAD 25 credit for >48h reschedules through Nov 30.
3) Require daily top-25 SKU cycle count in all district stores.

**08:30-10:30 | Disagreement segment**

- Jordan: "Without weather-flex slots, we cancel safety-critical installs when surge hits."
- Amrita: "Can we conditionally release one slot by 11:00 if weather radar risk is low?"
- Sofia: "Let's trial one protected slot + one conditional slot for 2 weeks."

**10:30-12:00 | Action items**
- Owner: Jordan | Due: 11 Nov | Publish revised appointment triage script with weather-flex logic.
- Owner: Amrita | Due: 10 Nov | Send district summary on inventory risk by tire size.
- Owner: Priya | Due: 12 Nov | Share cycle-count control checklist.
- Owner: David | Due: 10 Nov | Draft SMS template for schedule changes.

**12:00-13:30 | Unresolved question**
How should district fund expedited courier overages if snowfall causes repeated supplier delays (shared pool vs store-level chargeback)?

**13:30-14:30 | Sofia close**
We will close the courier-funding question next call after Finance feedback. Please post daily updates in the district Teams channel by 16:30 local.
